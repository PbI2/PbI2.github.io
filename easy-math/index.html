Problem 01 · A warm-up

Why is the sum of the first $n$ odd numbers equal to $n^2$?

Try arranging dots into successive squares. The first odd number adds the first row, the next odd number adds an L-shaped border, and each step creates one larger square.

$$1 + 3 + 5 + \cdots + (2n-1) = n^2.$$
