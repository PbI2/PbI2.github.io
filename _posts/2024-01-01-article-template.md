## A section heading

Inline mathematics uses single dollar signs, for example $E = mc^2$ or $\alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\beta^2}}$.

Display mathematics uses double dollar signs:

$$
\eta = \frac{P_{\mathrm{out}}}{P_{\mathrm{in}}}
  = \frac{J_{\mathrm{sc}} V_{\mathrm{oc}} FF}{P_{\mathrm{in}}}.
$$

You can also use LaTeX environments:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} &= \rho, \\
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} &= 0, \\
\nabla \times \mathbf{E} &= -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Use `\mathrm{}` for upright units and `\mathbf{}` for vectors. MathJax is loaded by the Jekyll layout, so formulas render in the browser without a build-time LaTeX dependency.
